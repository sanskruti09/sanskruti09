### What I've shipped

#### 🤖 AI Mock Interview System
A full-stack interview simulator that reads your resume + job description,
generates tailored questions, conducts a live voice interview via WebSockets,
and produces a scored assessment report with behavioral analysis.

`Gemini AI` `Google STT/TTS` `WebSockets` `FastAPI` `React` `OpenCV`

> CV–JD semantic matching · real-time streaming · timestamped transcripts · 25% better assessment precision

---

#### 📦 Laibel — AI Packaging Validation Platform
Production microservice powering automated quality control for packaging and labels.
Validates OCR text, barcodes, QR codes, colours, fonts, logos, trademarks — end to end.

`FastAPI` `Azure AI Document Intelligence` `OpenCV` `GPT-4` `Claude` `PostgreSQL` `MongoDB` `Redis` `Celery`

> Reduced manual QA effort by **80%** · async processing · dual-DB architecture · full observability with Langfuse

---

#### 💬 Knowledge Assistant — RAG Chatbot
Enterprise chatbot that ingests PDFs, stores them in a vector DB, and answers questions
with GPT-4. Highlights the exact source section in the document after every answer.

`LangChain` `GPT-4` `PGVector` `Flask JWT` `AWS S3` `React` `Google DocAI`

> Conversational accuracy boosted by **60%** · in-document source highlighting · OCR + translation support

---

`CrewAI` `Google Cloud STT` `Flask` `Python`

`OpenCV` `YOLOv5` `Mediapipe` `Dlib` `Gaze360` `Python`
